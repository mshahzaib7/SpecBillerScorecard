The 90-day method
Your report numbers cover the last 3 full months, which we treat as 90 days. This is the standard 3-month method for days in AR. Yearly figures multiply the 3 months by 4.
Formulas
Net charges
charges − contractual adjustments
Net ratio
net charges ÷ charges
Average daily charges
charges ÷ 90
payments × 100 ÷ net charges
total AR × 90 ÷ charges
AR older than 90 days × 100 ÷ total AR
claims denied × 100 ÷ claims submitted
claims paid on the first try × 100 ÷ claims submitted

Each estimate is compared with the Good target and with the top-performer target. Estimates are rounded to the nearest $100 and are never added together.

Grading
Scores move in a straight line between anchors and stop at 0 and 100. A score of 85 or more is Good, 60 up to 85 is Watch, and below 60 is Problem. The overall score is the weighted average of the numbers you entered, so a missing number never counts as zero. Letters: A 90+, B 80+, C 70+, D 60+, F below 60. With fewer than 3 numbers we don't give a grade.
Source
Targets are commonly cited industry benchmarks for US physician practices. 'Top performer' reflects best-in-class levels. Benchmarks vary by specialty and payer mix.
